01 THE PROBLEM

AI can sound authoritative.
That doesn't make it lawful.

In legal research, fluency is not authority. A useful answer must let practitioners, researchers, and citizens inspect the record behind it.

01 / ACCURACY

Fabricated or outdated sections

A convincing section number can still be repealed, amended, or unrelated to the underlying legal issue.

02 / AUTHORITY

Invented rules & blended acts

Generic models often merge provisions across central and state laws or invent rules no source document supports.

03 / EVIDENCE

Unverifiable legal answers

Plausible prose without page numbers, subsections, and inspectable excerpts is unacceptable in legal work.

03 HOW IT WORKS

From question
to verifiable evidence.

A disciplined four-stage pipeline separating user queries, untrusted document excerpts, and verified legal citations.

  1. 01 / INPUT Ask in plain languageBegin with the question.

    Frame an Indian law or policy question naturally. The research trail starts with what needs to be established—not a guessed section number.

    QUESTION → SEARCH INTENT
  2. 02 / RETRIEVAL Find verified recordsSearch the source set.

    Search only records marked verified, then keep the retrieved passage tied to its source and check for conflicting versions.

    SEARCH → VERIFIED PASSAGE
  3. 03 / VALIDATION Test the claimKeep support in view.

    Compare the response with retrieved text, including its numbers, negation, and legal references. A matching keyword alone is not the whole argument.

    PASSAGE → CLAIM CHECK
  4. 04 / PROVENANCE Inspect—or stopFollow the citation.

    Trace the answer to its document, section, page, and excerpt. If the record cannot support the response, the gap should remain explicit.

    CLAIM → SOURCE LOCATOR
