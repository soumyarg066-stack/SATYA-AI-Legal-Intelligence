01 / WHY SATYA EXISTS

Confidence isn't the same as correctness.

General-purpose AI systems routinely produce fluent, authoritative-sounding legal summaries that misquote statutory sections, overlook amendments, or invent non-existent rules. In Indian legal research, a plausible falsehood is worse than no answer at all.

SATYA is designed as an evidence-grounded architecture: retrieved source material is treated strictly as untrusted data rather than executable instructions, claims must link back to verified database records, and missing or conflicting evidence triggers an explicit abstention.

“AI must support human legal reasoning through traceable provenance—never replace official statutes, notifications, or qualified legal judgment.”
02 / DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Built around four non-negotiable standards.

Evidence-led

Every factual statement must cite an explicitly verified source record in the database.

Inspectable

Document titles, sections, page numbers, and verbatim excerpts remain visible side-by-side.

Honest about uncertainty

Missing records, conflicting passages, or unverified currentness are disclosed plainly.

Injection-resistant

Quoted legal text and conversation history are isolated from system instructions.

03 / VERIFICATION ARCHITECTURE

How SATYA handles legal edge cases.

Scenario Implemented Safeguard Outcome
Unverified uploads Retrieval queries filter strictly on verification_status = 'verified'. Excluded until verified
Conflicting provisions Detects differing text across documents for the same Act/section. Abstains & surfaces sources
Currentness queries Checks commencement date and amendment metadata before answering. Abstains if unverified
Unsupported claims Screens lexical overlap, numeric values, and negation polarity per claim. Withholds unsupported output
Important notice

SATYA is intended to support access to legal information, not to provide personalized legal advice or replace a qualified legal professional. Always consult official gazette publications, court records, and professional counsel for your specific circumstances.
